Scientific Scope & Limitations

Biologically Motivated: Coupled short-range WNT/EDAR activation vs. long-range BMP inhibition (Glover et al., 2023); truncated hair-placode program lacking mesenchymal condensate; multi-site wave initiation from the volar pad apex, dorsal-ventral nail boundary, and distal flexion crease.

Phenomenological Approximation: Consolidates multi-gene WNT/EDAR/BMP pathways into a 2-field cubic activator-inhibitor PDE with wave-guided nematic surface anisotropy; does not simulate 3D mesenchymal mechanics, week-16 sweat gland budding, or week-17 secondary ridges.

Research Integrity, Mathematical Formulation & Biological Bounds

1. Primary Biological Reference (Glover et al., 2023)

Glover, J. D., Sudderick, Z. R., Shih, B. B.-J., et al. (2023). The Developmental Basis of Fingerprint Pattern Formation and Variation. Cell, 186(5), 940–956.e20. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2023.01.015 | PubMed 36764291 | Open-Access Full Text PDF.

Human primary epidermal ridges initiate around gestational week 13 on the raised volar pads of the ventral distal phalanx (P3). Glover et al. established that fingerprint ridges execute a truncated hair follicle (HF) developmental program: nascent ridges express early epithelial placode markers (EDAR, WNT/LEF1/β-catenin, FGF20, BMP2, and selective mitogen TGFA) beneath a precociously differentiated KERATIN14+ suprabasal layer, but fail to express SHH and fail to recruit a SOX2+/DKK1+ mesenchymal dermal condensate because ventral digit mesenchyme lacks LEF1/WNT responsiveness.

2. Turing Reaction-Diffusion & Reduced Two-Field Formulation

Unlike generic Gray-Scott models (which rely on cubic autocatalysis U V2 unrelated to epidermal morphogens), this laboratory implements a two-field activator-inhibitor Turing PDE around basal equilibrium (A0, I0) with deviations u = A − A0, v = I − I0: 

∂A/∂t = D_A ∇²A + ∇·(D_aniso ∇A) + c(x,y,t) [ k_A u + μ_EDAR u² − γ u³ − k_IB v ] + S_init(x,y,t)
∂I/∂t = D_I ∇²I + c(x,y,t) [ k_AI u − k_I v ]

What is combined/abstracted: The mutual positive feedback between epithelial WNT/β-catenin signaling and EDAR expression is combined into the short-range Activator field A (DA ≪ DI). Secreted BMP ligands (BMP2/BMP7 acting via phospho-SMAD1/5) are represented by the fast-diffusing Inhibitor field I. The quadratic coefficient μEDAR captures the stripe-to-spot transition observed when EDAR signaling is ablated in EdaTa mutants (Glover et al., Fig. 4E).

3. Anatomical Initiation Sites & Wave Confluence

Ridges do not emerge simultaneously from uniform noise. Instead, patterning waves spread from three anatomical initiation zones on the digit: (1) the volar pad apex/center, (2) the dorsal-ventral (nail-bed) boundary, and (3) the distal digital flexion crease. Prominent symmetrical volar pads favor early central target waves that collide with boundary fronts to form whorls; intermediate asymmetrical pads yield loops; low regressed pads allow boundary waves to sweep uninterrupted across the digit as arches.

4. What This Simulation Cannot Establish

This 2D reaction-diffusion instrument models primary epidermal pre-patterning (weeks 13–15). It does not model 3D biomechanical buckling of the basal lamina, ongoing P3 digit elongation that stretches ridge spacing after layout, week-16 sweat gland budding, or week-17 secondary ridge insertion.
